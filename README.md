# 🎵 Music Player

A modern offline music player built with **Flutter** for playing, organizing, and managing music stored locally on your Android device.

## ✨ Features

- 🎧 **Offline Music Playback** — Play local music without an internet connection
- 📚 **Music Library** — Browse songs stored on your device
- 🔍 **Search** — Quickly find songs in your music library
- ❤️ **Favorites** — Save and manage favorite songs
- 📂 **Playlists** — Create and manage custom playlists
- 🎚️ **Playback Queue** — View, reorder, and play upcoming songs
- ▶️ **Next & Previous** — Navigate through the active playback queue
- 🔀 **Shuffle** — Randomize playback
- 🔁 **Repeat** — Repeat songs or the complete playback queue
- 🎛️ **Mini Player** — Persistent playback controls while navigating the app
- 🎵 **Now Playing** — Dedicated full-screen playback experience
- 🖼️ **Album Artwork** — Display and manage song artwork
- 🌙 **Dark & Light Themes** — Modern themes for different environments
- 🔔 **Media Notification** — Control playback directly from Android notifications
- 📱 **Background Playback** — Continue listening while using other apps
- 🗂️ **Song Management** — Rename and delete local music files
- 💾 **Playback Context** — Continue the last playback context without restoring playback position
- 📱 **Native Android Playback** — Dedicated native playback service for reliable background audio

## 🚀 Highlights

- Fully offline music experience
- Native Android background audio playback
- Android MediaStore integration
- Reorderable playback queue
- Playlist-aware playback
- Repeat All and Shuffle support
- Persistent mini player
- Local song and artwork management
- Dark and light theme support
- Smooth and responsive UI
